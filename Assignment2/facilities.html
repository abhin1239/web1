Beyond equipment

A good learning space is about people, too.

Facilities create the best outcomes when students have clear lab guidance, responsible access and support to ask questions. We encourage safe, respectful and purposeful use of every shared resource.

  • Follow lab rules and equipment instructions
  • Protect accounts, data and shared devices
  • Work collaboratively and respect others’ time
  • Report technical issues to the appropriate staff
