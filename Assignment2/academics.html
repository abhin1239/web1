Curriculum snapshot

What you’ll learn.

A sample four-stage learning journey. Your official syllabus may differ.

YEAR 01

Foundations

Programming basics, mathematics for computing, digital logic, communication and problem-solving.

YEAR 02

Core computer science

Data structures, algorithms, object-oriented programming, databases and computer architecture.

YEAR 03

Systems & specialisation

Operating systems, networks, software engineering, security, AI and technical electives.

YEAR 04

Integration & impact

Capstone project, internship or industry exposure, advanced electives and professional practice.

PRACTICE

Laboratory learning

Apply concepts through coding exercises, experiments, team assignments and demonstrations.

PROGRESSION

Career & higher studies

Build technical portfolios and prepare for software roles, research pathways and further study.

Learning outcomes

Graduate ready to reason, build and collaborate.

Our intended learning approach helps students connect theory to practice and explain the reasoning behind their solutions.

  • Analyse problems and design appropriate algorithms
  • Develop, test and maintain software applications
  • Work with data, systems and networks
  • Communicate technical ideas and collaborate effectively
  • Consider security, accessibility, ethics and social impact
