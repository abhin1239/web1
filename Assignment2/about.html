Who we are

Technology with purpose.
Education with impact.

The Department of B.Tech CSE nurtures thoughtful technologists who can understand complex problems and create useful, responsible solutions.

Our story

Curiosity is where progress begins.

At Federal Institute of Science and Technology, our department brings together foundational computer science, practical engineering and a spirit of exploration. Our learning environment is designed to help students move from understanding concepts to applying them with confidence.

Through collaborative projects, faculty guidance and continuous skill development, we prepare learners for changing technology landscapes and meaningful professional lives.

  • Conceptual clarity before shortcuts
  • Ethical, inclusive and human-centred innovation
  • Collaboration across disciplines
  • Continuous improvement through feedback
OUR VISION To inspire responsible innovators who use computing to improve lives.
OUR MISSION Teach deeply. Practise boldly. Serve thoughtfully.
